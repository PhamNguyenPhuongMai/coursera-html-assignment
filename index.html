Traditional Vietnamese dishes

Welcome to A famous traditional Vietnamese dish.

List of popular dishes

  • Pho

    Pho is a traditional Vietnamese noodle soup with flavorful broth, beef or chicken, rice noodles, and fresh herbs

  • Banh xeo

    Banh Xeo is a crispy yellow pancake filled with shrimp, pork, and bean sprouts, often served with fresh herbs.

  • Com tam

    Com Tam is a popular Southern Vietnamese dish made with broken rice, grilled pork, and fish sauce.

  • Bun bo Hue

    Bun Bo Hue is a spicy beef noodle soup from central Vietnam, known for its rich broth and lemongrass aroma.

  • Banh mi

    Banh Mi is a crispy baguette filled with meat, pate, fresh vegetables, and flavorful sauces.

  • Bun cha

    Bun Cha is a Hanoi specialty featuring grilled pork, rice noodles, fresh herbs, and sweet and sour fish sauce.
